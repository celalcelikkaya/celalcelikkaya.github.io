Chrome extension

Delete & Edit Element

Hide ads and popups or rewrite any text on any web page. Click an element to remove it or edit its text, and undo any time. Changes are saved per site and only you see them.

FreePrivateWorks on every site
4.67 rating · 36 users (Oct 2026)
View on Chrome Web Store

Remove ads, banners and popups

Turn on Remove mode, hover over anything and click. It disappears. Undo any time with Ctrl+Z.

Clean pages, remembered

Your changes are saved on your computer and applied again on every visit. Nothing leaves your computer.

Rewrite any text on any page

Click a piece of text, type, press Enter. Handy for screenshots and mock-ups.

Two clear choices

No settings to learn. Pick Remove something or Change some text, then click on the page. Undo last and Clear site are one click away.

Chrome extension

SSH Quick Commands

Save the SSH commands you run often, then copy them with one click on Google Cloud SSH, AWS, or any site. Group them, search them, no account needed.

FreePrivateNo account

Copy a command in one click

Click a saved command and it is already on your clipboard. No retyping, no digging through shell history.

Shows up on your sites

Add ssh.cloud.google.com, AWS or any console you use and a small Commands button appears every time, no toolbar click needed.

Organize into groups

Production, database, deploy: keep everything sorted and easy to find. Edit it all in your browser, and export or import a backup any time.

Add any site in one click

Chrome extension

All Wallet

A private address book for crypto wallets and bank accounts. Every entry is tagged with its network and asset, copies in one click, and seals itself after saving so a stray click cannot change it.

FreePrivateNo network requests
View on Chrome Web Store

Tagged by network and asset

Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Bitcoin and more. Every entry carries its network and asset, so you never send USDC down the wrong chain.

Bank accounts too

Pick Bank as the network and one of over 60 currencies, many of them African. Account number, bank name and account holder each copy separately.

Wallets seal themselves

Once an address is saved the row locks. A red open padlock means it can still be edited, a green closed one means it is sealed. Unlocking takes a deliberate confirmation.

Optional password lock

Turn on a password and the panel stays hidden until you unlock it. It locks again after an hour of inactivity, and the check happens on your device.

Web app

Papa Radio

A live radio web app with over 50,000 stations from 200+ countries, plus Papa TV for kids and a Netflix-style TV Mode. Installable on your phone or computer.

FreeInstallable3 modes
50K+ stations · 200+ countries
Open paparadio.online

50K+ stations, 200+ countries

Browse live FM and online stations from around the world. Filter by country or genre, or search by name. A mic button lets you search by voice.

One tap from music

Open the site and you are one tap from a live station. Once it plays, a player bar stays at the bottom with previous, next, volume, favorite and share.

A Netflix-style TV Mode

Switch to a big-screen layout with a side rail, a full-width hero and rows like Trending Now. Made for a TV or a laptop on the couch.

Papa TV for kids

A separate mode with 95 live kids channels, a clean player, a searchable list and a strip of more channels to explore.

Papa TV in TV Mode too

The same big-screen layout works for kids channels, with a Recently Watched row and a side rail to jump between TV, radio, favorites and history.

Installable, with Drive Mode

Install it as a web app on your phone or computer. Drive Mode gives a simpler layout for use in the car, and the heart on any station or channel saves it to Favorites.
